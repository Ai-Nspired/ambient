# Pulse — what it is, for the record

`justice`'s own words, kept here rather than in the first pass: the onboarding teaches
the wheel, not the architecture.

## The role
Pulse is the ambient assistant in this medium. It takes the work ARA used to do: taking
in preferences and intents — "our intents are explicit are explicit" — and holding them
as part of the learning loop.

- Explicit intent stays explicit. Nothing inferred is promoted to confirmed.
- What he says goes to Pulse. Pulse works out what he is asking for and helps him do it
  in the ecosystem: connected things it can reach, things it can do on his behalf.
- What comes back may become cards. Notes will end up as cards too, and those go into
  the system as well.
- This is the operator's description of the loop, not something this page implements.
  Nothing on this page writes a preference store or a card index.

## Deliberately not in the onboarding
The first pass says who she is — "I am your ambient assistant… ambient does not mean
passive" — and teaches the wheel: PTT, frequency, key, recordings, Settings. It does not
say that Pulse does ARA's work, and it does not explain the card system. Those are
internal, and the operator gives them; the agent does not lecture them back.
